Research foundation & future directions.

This page separates the founding PI's pre-IITG research experience from projects that will be initiated under HECS at IIT Guwahati.

The funded projects below describe Dr. Dhananjay Kumar's research work before joining IIT Guwahati. They are included to show the technical foundation of HECS and are not presented as IIT Guwahati grants.
PI research foundation

Selected ongoing/pre-IITG work.

University of Wisconsin–Madison · hydrogen safety

Hydrogen leak detection technique development

Funding context: ARPA-E, U.S. Department of Energy project at UW–Madison.

Simulation of hydrogen leak scenarios and development of optical leak-detection approaches, using CONVERGE CFD to connect release physics with real-time detection.

University of Wisconsin–Madison · thermal safety

Engine & hybrid-system powertrain modelling

Funding context: Ford Motor Company project at UW–Madison.

Thermal runaway modelling of Li-ion batteries in hybrid powertrains using CONVERGE CFD, user-defined functions and electrochemical/thermal submodels.

HECS at IITG

Initial project directions.

These are research directions for lab development, not claims of awarded funding.

Direction 01

Hydrogen release regimes and safety

Validated CFD/experiments for buoyancy-, momentum- and wind-dominated hydrogen releases.

Direction 02

Methanol, ammonia and natural gas

Ignition, stability and emissions across the low-carbon fuels, with hydrogen as the promoter that makes the slow ones usable.

Direction 03

Hydrogen direct injection and advanced ignition

Spark, multi-point and pre-chamber concepts, and how they interact with hydrogen direct injection, alternative fuels and sprays.
