HECS IITG announced ahead of a November 2026 start

The Hydrogen, Energy and Combustion Systems Laboratory is being established at the School of Energy Science and Engineering, IIT Guwahati. This site sets out the research themes, the facilities roadmap and the first-cohort openings ahead of the PI's joining. Enquiries from prospective PhD, MS(R) and undergraduate researchers are open now — see Join Us.

2026
Patent

Indian patent granted for a high-pressure coaxial direct injection system

Indian Patent 589697, "High-pressure coaxial direct injection system for an internal combustion engine" (A. K. Agarwal, H. Valera, D. Kumar), has been granted. The injection concept underpins several of the methanol locomotive-engine studies that form part of the HECS research foundation.

2026
Publication

Low-carbon fuel jets in DISI engine-like conditions published in Fuel

"Numerical modeling of macroscopic and dynamic jet characteristics of low-carbon fuels: H₂, CNG, and blends in DISI engine-like conditions" (Q. Pham, D. Kumar, A. K. Agarwal, S. Park) extends the hydrogen and natural-gas jet work into blended low-carbon fuels.

2024
Publication

Laser ignition compared against spark ignition for hydrogen-enriched natural gas

Published in Applied Thermal Engineering, this constant-volume combustion chamber study contrasts laser and conventional spark ignition across hydrogen-enriched natural gas–air mixtures — directly feeding the advanced-ignition direction HECS will pursue at IITG.
