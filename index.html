Research philosophy

Understand the governing physics, validate it experimentally, then translate it into cleaner and safer systems.

The lab will be built around questions that require both fundamental transport science and engineering-scale interpretation.

Founding PI

Dr. Dhananjay Kumar

Assistant Professor · School of Energy Science and Engineering · Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati

Principal Investigator

Experiments, modelling and cleaner propulsion.

The research foundation behind HECS spans hydrogen-enriched combustion, hydrogen and natural-gas jets, alternative-fuel sprays, laser ignition, engine modelling, hydrogen leak dispersion and Li-ion battery thermal safety.

Before joining IIT Guwahati, Dr. Kumar's research included work at the Engine Research Center, University of Wisconsin–Madison, and doctoral research at IIT Kanpur.
