HECS IITG / Facilities
Facilities

Build capability around the science.

HECS is a new laboratory. This page intentionally distinguishes immediate computational capability from planned experimental infrastructure.

Initial capability

Computational modelling

CFD, reactive/species transport, multiphase modelling, heat transfer and reduced-order analysis. Software access will depend on IITG licensing and sponsored projects.

Planned build

Hydrogen flow & safety bench

Controlled leak/jet experiments, mass-flow control, concentration sensing, ventilation studies and optical flow visualization.

Planned build

Combustion & ignition platform

Constant-volume/optical combustion capability for spark/laser ignition, flame imaging and gaseous/low-carbon fuel studies.

Growth phase

Engine & fuel systems

Instrumented engine research, pressure measurement, fuel injection and alternative-fuel conversion depending on funded project scope.

Growth phase

Advanced diagnostics

High-speed imaging, Schlieren/shadowgraph systems and selected laser diagnostics as the laboratory matures.

Growth phase

Thermal safety

Bench-scale thermal-management and ignition/safety experiments for batteries and hybrid energy systems.

Development roadmap

Three capability phases.

Phase 01

CFD + foundational bench experiments

Workstations/software, gas handling, flow control, sensors, high-speed imaging and safe small-scale hydrogen experiments.

Phase 02

Combustion, ignition and optical diagnostics

Optical chamber work, flame/spray diagnostics, controlled ignition and broader sustainable-fuel capability.

Phase 03

Integrated engine, hydrogen-safety and thermal systems

Larger experiments and application-scale systems developed through sponsored research and collaboration.
