Facilities

Computational modelling and planned experimental facilities at IIT Guwahati.

Available now

Students at HECS can use the following software for computational research.

  • Ansys for engineering simulation.
  • MATLAB for numerical modelling and data analysis.
  • CONVERGE for computational fluid dynamics.

IIT Guwahati shared resources

Institute software

The Computer and Communication Centre lists Overleaf for collaborative scientific writing and MathType for equation editing. Check the institute software catalogue for licence dates and the campus software repository.

High-performance computing

IIT Guwahati operates the PARAM Ishan and PARAM Kamrupa supercomputers and a separate GPU computing facility. Students planning large simulations can discuss their requirements with the PI and use the IIT Guwahati HPC portal for account requests and system-specific support.

Planned experimental facilities

Planned build

Hydrogen flow & safety bench

Controlled leak/jet experiments, mass-flow control, concentration sensing, ventilation studies and optical flow visualization.

Planned build

Spray & combustion chamber

A constant-volume chamber with optical access and a single-injector mount: spray penetration and evaporation, spark and pre-chamber ignition, flame imaging and pressure-trace analysis across hydrogen, natural gas, methanol and ammonia.

Growth phase

Hydrogen & alternative-fuel engine

An instrumented single-cylinder engine for hydrogen direct injection and alternative-fuel operation, with in-cylinder pressure measurement, injection control and fuel-system conversion, as sponsored project scope allows.

Growth phase

Advanced diagnostics

High-speed imaging, Schlieren and shadowgraph systems, and pressure-based diagnostics as the laboratory matures.

Growth phase

Thermal safety

Bench-scale thermal-management and ignition/safety experiments for batteries and hybrid energy systems.
