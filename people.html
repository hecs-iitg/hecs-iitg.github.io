Founding Principal Investigator

Dr. Dhananjay Kumar

Incoming Assistant Professor
School of Energy Science and Engineering
Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati
Joining November 2026

Research interests: hydrogen combustion and safety, low-carbon fuels, spray and ignition physics, internal-combustion engines, computational fluid dynamics and thermal safety.

Dr. Kumar completed his M.Tech + PhD in Mechanical Engineering at IIT Kanpur and subsequently worked at the Engine Research Center, University of Wisconsin–Madison. His research bridges controlled experiments with CFD and system-level interpretation.
