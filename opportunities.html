Indicative first-cohort projects

Three problems, described properly.

These are the directions the lab will open with. Scope is adjusted to the student and to what gets funded — but this is the substance, not a topic list.

Project 01 · PhD or MS(R)

When does a hydrogen leak stop behaving like a jet?

The question
An accidental hydrogen release starts as an under-expanded momentum-driven jet and ends as a buoyant plume steered by ventilation or wind. Where that transition happens decides where the flammable envelope sits — and therefore where a sensor has to be to catch it in time. Current guidance treats the regimes separately.
Year one
Build and validate a CFD case for a compressible hydrogen release against published jet data; map the transition across release pressure, orifice size and crosswind; identify the dimensionless grouping that predicts it.
You would learn
Compressible and buoyant flow modelling, species transport, mesh and AMR strategy for near-field jets, and systematic verification and validation.
Good fit if
You are comfortable with fluid mechanics and want to do computation properly — not just run a solver.
Project 02 · PhD

Making ammonia and hydrogen burn stably together

The question
Ammonia is attractive to store and awful to ignite; hydrogen is the reverse. Blending them is the obvious move, but the blend that ignites reliably and the blend that keeps NOx down are not the same blend. The trade-off is not yet well characterised at engine-relevant conditions.
Year one
Chemical-kinetic analysis of ignition delay and laminar flame speed across blend ratio, pressure and temperature; then constant-volume chamber experiments on flame-kernel growth as the chamber capability comes online.
You would learn
Combustion chemistry and mechanism handling, ignition and flame-propagation measurement, high-speed and Schlieren imaging, emissions analysis.
Good fit if
You want both sides — the kinetics and the hardware — rather than only one.
Project 03 · PhD or MS(R)

Ignition systems that can keep up with low-carbon fuels

The question
Conventional spark ignition was designed around gasoline. Hydrogen pre-ignites; ammonia barely lights. Laser and pre-chamber ignition both change the earliest milliseconds of the event — and those milliseconds set everything that follows. Which concept suits which fuel, and why, is still largely empirical.
Year one
Characterise flame-kernel development under spark versus laser ignition for a chosen fuel, in a controlled chamber; connect kernel behaviour to the subsequent pressure trace and to a reduced model that a designer could actually use.
You would learn
Optical diagnostics, ignition-system instrumentation, high-speed image processing, and reduced-order model building from experimental data.
Good fit if
You like careful experiments and are prepared to build the measurement, not just take it.

Funding & eligibility

The questions everyone emails to ask.

Is the PhD funded?

Full-time PhD students at IIT Guwahati are normally supported through an institute assistantship, subject to eligibility and the institute's current rules. Project-funded positions may also open as sponsored research begins. Exact eligibility, amounts and duration are set by the institute, not by the lab — check the official pages before applying.

IITG admissions Doctoral programmes

What about PMRF?

The Prime Minister's Research Fellowship is a separate, competitive national route with its own eligibility and deadlines, and is open to strong candidates at IITG. If you are considering it, say so in your first email — the preparation timeline is longer than a normal application.

PMRF

Do I need prior hydrogen or CFD experience?

No. Fundamentals, evidence that you can learn something difficult on your own, and clear technical writing matter more. Coding ability helps in every project here. Experimental discipline is teachable; carelessness is not.

When should I apply?

IIT Guwahati runs its PhD admissions in cycles with dates set by the institute — check the admissions page for the current advertisement. Because HECS starts in November 2026, contacting the PI ahead of the cycle you are targeting is worthwhile.
